We'll be camping at Vega State Park, hanging out by the campfire, and enjoying ad hoc rides up onto the Grand Mesa National Forest that directly borders the State Park!

We'll set up camp Friday August 21st, and stay through Sunday afternoon August 23rd. Most of us will be staying in the Early Settlers Campground in Vega State Park, Reservations are highly recommended as it is a popular park. It is legal to ride your stickered OHV directly from your campsite out to the trails. There is dispersed National Forest camping a few miles up the nearby Park Creek Rd if you prefer, but be warned the road can be a little rough depending on usage and erosion if you choose this option!

Bring everything you need for a weekend of camping and riding. We'll hang out by the campfire in the evenings and organize ad hoc rides Saturday and Sunday for those interested. Bring the family to this one!
