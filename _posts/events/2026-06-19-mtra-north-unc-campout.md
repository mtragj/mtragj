Join us for a weekend of camping, hanging out around the campfire, and riding up on the Uncompahgre Plateau. Beat the growing summer heat by heading up to camp among  the Aspens.

We'll set up camp in the dispersed camping area in the meadow across the road from the Divide Forks Campground (see the map pin), you can choose to stay in the campground if you want (there is a USFS fee for those sites.)  Beginning Friday afternoon and camping through Sunday afternoon.

You can expect some solid bench racing around the campfire each night and ad hoc rides on the nearby trails, some of which start a couple hundred feet from the camping area.

Bring everything you need to ride and camp for a weekend: food, water, extra gas, shelter, etc.
