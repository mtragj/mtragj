The [Grand Valley Dirt Bike Rally](https://grandvalleydirtbikerally.com/) is back for 2026, put on by the MTRA to showcase our local trail systems and bring riders from across the region together.  The event area at the 27-1/4 Road MX Track is open all weekend, with guided rides rolling out Saturday and Sunday.

**Highlights**

- **[Guided rides](https://grandvalleydirtbikerally.com/rides/)** in Bangs Canyon, the North Desert, Rabbit Valley, and the GJ Desert, with a variety of lengths and difficulties from easy to advanced, including ladies' rides
- **[Trail Skills Enhancer](https://grandvalleydirtbikerally.com/rides/gj_desert/easy/skills-training-trail-skills-enhancer/)** - beginner/novice skills training on Friday afternoon
- **[Event area fun](https://grandvalleydirtbikerally.com/activities/)** - vendor expo, food trucks, campfire and s'mores, yard games, the Slow Ride Contest, and music or a movie
- **[Door prizes](https://grandvalleydirtbikerally.com/register/#door-prizes)** - every ride you sign up for is an entry to win a 2024 GASGAS Moto 1 E-Bike or a $250 Klim gift card.  The drawing is Saturday at 6pm and you must be present to win

Registration is through the [rally website](https://grandvalleydirtbikerally.com/register/), where you'll also find the full ride list and weekend activity schedule.  This is an AMA sanctioned event, so every rider must sign an AMA waiver in person at check-in.
