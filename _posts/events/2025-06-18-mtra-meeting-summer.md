Just a quick reminder that we are having our next quarterly meeting on the 18th of June, 6pm at Smokin Oak in Clifton.

We'll talk about our plans for upcoming Group Rides and Campouts this Summer/Fall, opportunities for volunteer trail work/maintenance, and get updates from our BLM and USFS partners on their work plans as well. 

 Last but not least, we'll bring everyone up to speed on the Grand Valley Dirt Bike Rally, 24-26 October! A full weekend of great rides, camping out, cool activities and lots of fun! It will be a blast!  

Everyone is welcome to the meeting - Bring your riding buddies, friends, spouses and kids!  Everybody loves pizza!
