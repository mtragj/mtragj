Pivot Brandz in downtown Grand Junction is hosting a _Throttle and Brew_ Bikes and Coffee style meetup in downtown Grand Junction this Saturday. This could be a fun gathering to meet other local bikers, and they'll make it a regular thing if this one has enough interest. Brave the cold! 

> Complimentary Palisade coffee, donuts and giveaways for the bike community.  Just a fun gathering for the moto enthusiasts in our area.

> If you are around, please stop by.  If you are here early enough you can park right outside of our shop in our zoned area. All bikes are welcome!  If this is a success, we will continue it in the spring when weather gets warmer.

> Hope to see ya here:)
