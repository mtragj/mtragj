Desert trail ride for this week's Thursday night Moto Meetup!

This week's trail ride will take us out to 21 road via the scenic route on single track alternating between ridgetops, washes, and hill climbs before dropping us onto 21 road briefly, we'll run down some fast single-track to the gas plant, and then head back east to blaze down some double-track to get us back to the parking lot before dark!

It's going to be hot, so bring lots of water and your warm weather gear!

Reminder that all Rides require signing a liability waiver, parents signatures required for riders under 18.
