Meet at the Corrals (see directions link) at 7:30pm, kickstands up at 8:00pm. Ride Sarlacc and the Star Wars trails at dusk, great views of the sunset over the Grand Valley as you wind around edge of the ridges on narrow bench cut single-track and over moderate rock obstacles.

Helmight light and/or strong headlight required, ride will not return to the trailhead until after dark!
