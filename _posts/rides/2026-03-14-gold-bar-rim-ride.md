Meet: 9:30 AM at the Seven Mile Parking (a.k.a. Gemini Bridges Trailhead)  [Seven Mile Parking](https://maps.app.goo.gl/3iv3LHg6hq2HR9w5A)

Kickstands Up: 10:00 AM

Parking: Free parking is available across the highway at Bar M or you can pay a fee to park at the Seven Mile lot ($5).

We will head up Gemini Bridges Road, then tackle Gold Bar Rim, Golden Spike, and Poison Spider. The ride ends at the Poison Spider Trailhead.  The ride will be approximately 40-50 miles.

There is a 10-mile pavement section to return to the Seven Mile Parking area. Riders should have street-legal bikes or arrange for a shuttle at the Poison Spider Trailhead. Alternative route options will be available if being street legal is an issue.
