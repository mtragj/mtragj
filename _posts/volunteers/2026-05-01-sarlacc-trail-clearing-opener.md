Meet just past the corrals (see the directions link!) by 9am, bring leather gloves, eye protection, and hand saws if you have them.

We'll ride up the Star Wars Trails with the BLM clearing deadfall and repairing trail tread that may have eroded over the winter wildlife closure. This is an opportunity to help give back to our local trails and get a preview of the trail system before the public opener on May 2nd.
